## Hi, I'm François-Xavier

I'm François-Xavier Cat, a cloud and platform engineer focused on Azure, CI/CD, automation, Terraform, PowerShell, and AI agents and APIs. LazyWinAdmin is where I share what I learn while solving technical problems through automation.

## What this blog covers

- **PowerShell**: scripts, modules, and practical tips
- **Azure**: cloud services, deployment, and operations
- **Terraform**: infrastructure as code
- **Automation and DevOps**: CI/CD pipelines and workflows that save time

## Background

I started my career as a network administrator at a small fintech startup, supporting everything from servers, desktops, and phones to databases, virtualization, and backups. Looking for ways to do more in less time, I got into automation in 2006 after reading [*Windows Server Hacks*](https://www.amazon.com/Windows-Server-Hacks-Mitch-Tulloch/dp/0596006470) by Mitch Tulloch, and started writing VBScript and Monad (PowerShell) scripts.

Working mostly with Microsoft and VMware systems, PowerShell became my language of choice, and I started this blog to share my code and solutions. That focus on automation led me to roles where I was often "the automation guy", and from there into DevOps and cloud engineering.
